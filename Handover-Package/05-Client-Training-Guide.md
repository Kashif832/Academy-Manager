## Welcome to Academy Manager

Academy Manager is your online office for running your academy — students, fees,
attendance, staff and your public website, all in one place, from any browser.

### Step 1 — Log in 📷 `01-tenant-login`
1. Go to the web address we gave you (e.g. `https://app.youracademy.com`).
2. Enter the **email** and **password** we provided.
3. Click **Sign in**. You'll see your **Dashboard**.
> First time? Go to **Settings → Change password** and set your own password.

### Step 2 — Your Dashboard 📷 `02-dashboard`
This is your home screen. At a glance you see how many students you have, how much
fee money you've collected this month, what's still outstanding, and attendance.

### Step 3 — Add your classes 📷 `05-classes`
1. Open **Classes**.
2. Click **Add class** — give it a name (e.g. "Grade 8"), a section, the monthly
   fee, and the teacher.
3. Repeat for each class. *(Do this before adding students.)*

### Step 4 — Add your students 📷 `03-students-list`, `04-student-add`
1. Open **Students** → **Add student**.
2. Fill in the student's name, the parent's name and phone, the class, and the
   monthly fee.
3. Save. The student now appears in your list, and their first fee invoice is ready.

### Step 5 — Mark attendance every day 📷 `06-attendance`
1. Open **Attendance**.
2. Choose the class and the date.
3. Tap each student **Present**, **Absent**, or **Late**, then **Save**.

### Step 6 — Collect and record fees 📷 `07-fees-overview`, `08-record-payment`
1. Open **Fees**. Each student shows a status: **Paid**, **Pending**, **Partial**,
   or **Overdue**.
2. When a parent pays, click the invoice → **Record payment** → enter the amount →
   Save.
3. A **receipt** 📷 `09-receipt-pdf` is created — you can print or share it.
> Made a mistake? You can **void or refund** a payment. The record is corrected but
> the history is always kept.

### Step 7 — See your reports 📷 `10-reports`
Open **Reports** for summaries of collections, outstanding dues and attendance.
Click **Export** to download a **PDF** for your records or a meeting.

### Step 8 — Your public website 📷 `11-website-cms`, `14-public-site`
1. Open **Website** to edit what the public sees about your academy.
2. Save — your page updates at your public link.
3. Parents can read about you and **send an admission inquiry** from there.

### Step 9 — Admission inquiries 📷 `12-admissions-inquiries`
Inquiries from your website arrive in **Admissions**. Open one to see the parent's
details and follow up.

### Step 10 — Settings 📷 `13-settings`
- **Change your password**, update your academy's details.
- **Add staff** (an assistant admin or a teacher) — each gets their own login.
- **Upgrade your plan** — send an upgrade request; we approve it and your new limits
  apply.

### Getting help
If something looks wrong, contact us (see the details we provided). We can securely
look at your academy from our side and help you directly.
